## Why This Exists

Not every automation needs a full scripting environment. Sometimes you only want to repeat a short, honest sequence: open the same folders, click through a dialog you use daily, or apply identical navigation in software you already run. Heavy tools can feel oversized when the job is simply “do what I just did again.”

**TinyTask** solves **one specific problem**: recording and replaying simple macros for repetitive tasks you perform on your own PC. Capture the path, save it if useful, and trigger playback when the chore returns — without building a project around a three-minute routine.

If you want the lightest path from “I keep doing this” to “let the PC repeat my steps,” **TinyTask** is for you. Use it for legitimate personal and work repetition only — never for cheating, bypassing protections, or harming systems you do not own.

The charm of **TinyTask** is restraint: a small window, a clear record button, and playback that does what you just demonstrated. When the chore is short and honest, that is often all the automation you need before returning to focused work at your own desk again.

## Quick Start with TinyTask

1. **Get TinyTask** — Download the compact Windows executable and place it in a folder you control.
2. **Launch** — Open **TinyTask**; the small recorder window is your entire workspace.
3. **Start recording** — Begin capture and perform the legitimate task you want repeated.
4. **Stop recording** — End capture in **TinyTask** once the sequence is complete.
5. **Replay once** — Run playback on a safe sample to confirm the macro still matches the UI.
6. **Save (optional)** — Store macros you will reuse into a local path you trust.
7. **Work normally** — Trigger **TinyTask** only when that same personal workflow returns.
